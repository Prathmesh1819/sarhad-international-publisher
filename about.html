About The Platform

Supporting student research, review, and publication.

Sarhad International Publisher is designed as a college-level digital publishing platform that gives students a complete research workflow from submission to public publication.

Our Mission

We aim to create a structured environment where student authors can submit scholarly work, receive administrative review, and share approved research with the wider academic community.

How It Works

Students log in, upload their research papers in PDF format, and track the review outcome. Admins evaluate each submission, add remarks, and publish approved papers to the public archive.

Platform Values

Why this system feels like a real publisher

Structured Submission

Articles include title, abstract, category, author details, and a PDF document upload.

Review-Based Approval

Admins can approve or reject submissions and add remarks to simulate an editorial process.

Public Publication

Only approved work appears on the public publications page for reading and downloading.

Academic Categories

Arts, Commerce, and Science are supported for undergraduate bachelor-level submissions.
